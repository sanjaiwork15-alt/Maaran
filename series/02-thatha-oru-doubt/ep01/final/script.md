Changes from the written directions (standing rules):
- No "ETHYLENE" label on screen (no on-screen text). Ethylene is a colourless gas, so it is drawn as faint, unlabelled
  shimmer rings; Thatha names it in the voice.
- Both hands hold bananas in Scenes 1, 2, 3 and 5, so the walking stick is left out of that waist-up shot (as in the
  Groundnut kitchen shot); on the thinnai it leans beside him.

## Fact check
- Bananas are **climacteric** fruits: they are harvested mature-green and keep ripening after picking. Ripening is
  driven by the plant hormone **ethylene**, which the fruit itself produces in a burst as ripening starts; the
  ethylene then speeds up ripening (it is autocatalytic) and also ripens fruit stored next to it.
  Sources: TNAU Agritech Portal, Post-harvest technology – Banana (ripening); ICAR-National Research Centre for
  Banana, banana ripening guidance; FSSAI guidance note "Artificial ripening of fruits – ethylene gas" (2018), which
  permits ethylene gas (up to 100 ppm) for ripening because it is the fruit's own ripening hormone.
- "Pazhukkura nerathula … ethylene veliya vidum … vegathai adhigamaakka udhavum" – correct as written (released while
  ripening; it speeds ripening, it does not make an unripe immature fruit ripen instantly).
- "Marathula irundhu" – everyday Tamil calls the banana plant "vaazha maram"; botanically it is a giant herb. Kept as
  the natural spoken word.
- Staring does not ripen fruit – humour, clearly a joke.
