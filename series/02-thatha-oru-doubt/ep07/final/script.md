## Fact check
- Many ants lay a trail pheromone (a chemical signal) as they walk back to the nest from food; nestmates detect it with
  their antennae and follow it. "Munnadi pona erumbu… vaasanai chemical-ai vittuttu pogum… pinthodarndhu pogum" – correct.
- Ants that find food reinforce the trail, so a good route gets a stronger signal; unused trails fade as the chemical
  evaporates. "Balamaana signal-a maaralaam" – correct, and the "maaralaam" (can become) keeps it honest: not every
  species forages this way. Sources: Hölldobler & Wilson, *The Ants* (Harvard University Press, 1990);
  Sumpter & Beekman, "From nonlinearity to optimality: pheromone trail foraging by ants", *Animal Behaviour* 66 (2003).
