## Fact check
- Harvested leafy herbs keep losing water by **transpiration** (mostly through the stomata, tiny pores on the leaves),
  but the cut stems no longer get water from the roots, so the leaves lose turgor and droop. Leafy greens look wilted
  after losing only about 3–5 % of their water. Sources: TNAU Agritech Portal, post-harvest handling of leafy
  vegetables; Kader (ed.), *Postharvest Technology of Horticultural Crops*, UC ANR (water loss, wilting thresholds).
- Heat, direct sun, dry air and air movement speed up water loss; cool, humid, covered storage slows it. So "don't
  leave it in the sun" and "if left open it wilts quickly" are correct; "store it properly" is deliberately general.
  Same sources (storage of coriander: low temperature, high relative humidity).
