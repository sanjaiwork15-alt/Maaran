bacteria geosmin-nu oru porulai uruvaakkum; mazhai thuli vizhumbodhu romba chinna thuligal andha vaasanaiya
kaathula namma mookku varaikkum kondu varum! Vivasaayikku mazhai sandhosham, Thatha-ku pazhaya ninaivugal 😄
Ungalukku man vaasanai vandhaa enna nyabagam varum? 👇
