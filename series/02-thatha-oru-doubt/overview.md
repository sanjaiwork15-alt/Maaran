# Series 02 – Thatha, Oru Doubt! (தாத்தா, ஒரு டவுட்டு!)

**Status:** Owner's master brief received 2026-10-19 (reference only); scripts come from the owner one by one.
**Format:** Everyday questions about food, plants, nature and simple science, answered by Thatha with experience,
simple science, relatable examples and a little humour. Not a formal lesson, not a facts channel.
**Tagline:** Every doubt has an answer. Every answer has a Thatha twist! / ஒவ்வொரு சந்தேகத்துக்கும் ஒரு பதில்… தாத்தா ஸ்டைலில்!
**Channel:** Maaran Thatha's Farm. **Platforms:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels.
**Episode length:** ~40–50 s. **Season 1:** 8 episodes.
**Location:** changes with the story (farm, thottam, kitchen, vayal…); Thatha's look never changes (`bible/character.md`).

## Fixed structure (flexible guide)
0–3 s curiosity hook (question or funny observation, no long intro) → 3–12 s relatable situation →
12–30 s Thatha explains (one clear, accurate explanation + visual demo) → 30–40 s funny Thatha moment →
40–50 s one specific, easy-to-answer comment question about the topic (vary the type: experience, preference,
household tip, local knowledge). No generic "like, share, subscribe" every time.

## Owner's rules for this series
- Dialogue in natural conversational Tanglish (owner records/reads Tanglish, not Tamil script); directions in English;
  titles in Tanglish + Tamil script. ("Tamil script" in `lines.json` is only for the voice engine.)
- Humour from expressions, comparisons, delivery – never from silly or wrong answers. Never preachy.
- Accuracy first: verify with reliable sources (TNAU for crops; food-science / horticulture references otherwise),
  separate facts from beliefs, no myths as facts, no exaggerated health claims, say honestly when it depends.
  Every script gets a fact-check section.
- Use the established 2D Thatha; expressive faces, gestures, close-ups and clear demonstrations.
- Viewer questions can become future episodes.
