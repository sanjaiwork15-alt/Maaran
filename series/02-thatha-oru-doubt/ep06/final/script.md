## Fact check
- Heat flows from the warmer body to the colder one; the rate grows with the temperature difference, so ice in warm
  water melts faster than in room-temperature water. Correct.
- While melting, ice takes in energy (latent heat of fusion) and changes from solid to liquid (its temperature stays at
  0 °C until it has melted) – "andha aatralai eduthukkumbodhu, thidamaana nilaiyilirundhu thirava nilaiya maarudhu"
  is correct. Sources: NCERT Class 9 Science, ch. 1 "Matter in Our Surroundings" (change of state, latent heat);
  NCERT Class 7 Science, "Heat" (transfer of heat from hotter to colder).
- Ice floats (less dense than water) – the animation shows the cubes floating. Melted ice dilutes the juice – correct.
