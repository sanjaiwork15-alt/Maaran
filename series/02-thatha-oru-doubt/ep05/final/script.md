## Fact check
- Paddy (nel) is the rice grain still inside its husk (hull: the lemma and palea); milling removes the husk (brown rice)
  and polishing removes the bran to give white rice. "Vayalla arisi vellaiyaa irukkaadhu… veliya oru thol" – correct.
- The order harvest → threshing (grain separated from the straw) → drying (to about 14 % moisture, safe for storage and
  milling) → milling is the standard post-harvest chain. Harvest when about 80 % of the grains in a panicle are straw-
  coloured. Sources: TNAU Agritech Portal, Rice – post-harvest technology; IRRI Rice Knowledge Bank (harvesting,
  threshing, drying, milling).
