## Fact check
- Roots need oxygen from the air spaces between soil particles; overwatering fills those spaces with water, roots
  run short of air and can rot – "mannula kaathoda idam koraiyalaam; vergalukkum prachanai varalaam": correct and
  carefully hedged ("-laam", can). Sources: RHS, "Watering plants" (rhs.org.uk); University of Minnesota Extension,
  "Watering indoor plants"; TNAU Agritech portal, nursery/pot culture notes on drainage.
- "Mannoda eerathai paarthu, thevaippadumbodhu thanni oothanum": the standard advice – feel the soil about a finger
  joint deep and water when it is dry; pots need drainage holes and should not stand in water.
- "Dhinamum thanni oothanum… appadithaaney?" is set up as the mistake and corrected – not presented as advice.
