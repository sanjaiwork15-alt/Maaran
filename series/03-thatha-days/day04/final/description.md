**Title:** Vidhaiya Paarthu Mudivu Panna Mudiyaadhu! 🌱 | விதைய பார்த்து முடிவு பண்ண முடியாது! | Maaran Thatha – Day 4

**Description:**
Indha chinna vidhaikkulla oru periya chedi irukku-nu sonnaa nambuveengalaa? 🌱 Eeram, veppam, kaathu – moonum
sariyaa irundhaa dhaan vidhai mulaikkum. Mudhal-la ver, appuram thalir… konjam konjamaa dhaan valarchi!
Nalla vishayangalukkum konjam neramum gavanippum thevai – Maaran Thatha sonna maadhiri 😊
Neenga kadaisiyaa vidhaichu valartha chedi edhu? Sollunga 👇
