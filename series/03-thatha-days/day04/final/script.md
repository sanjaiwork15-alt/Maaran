## Fact check
- Seeds need moisture, a suitable temperature and oxygen (air in the soil) to germinate – "sariyaana eeram, veppam,
  kaathoda soozhal… ellame thevai": correct. (TNAU Agritech Portal, groundnut sowing; standard seed-germination
  biology: water uptake/imbibition, temperature, O2 for respiration.)
- "Mudhal-la ver valarum, appuram thalir veliya varum": correct – the radicle (first root) emerges first, then the
  shoot comes up. Groundnut emerges in about a week or so depending on soil moisture and temperature.
- "Ovvoru naalum thevaiyaana gavanippu kidaichaa, nalla valara vaaippu adhigam" – careful wording ("vaaippu
  adhigam", more chance), no promise.
