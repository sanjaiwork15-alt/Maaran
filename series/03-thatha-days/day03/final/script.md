# Maaran Thatha — Day 3
**Thatha-voda Thookkamum Kozhi Koovuradhum!** 😴🐓

Owner's script (2026-10-28), Thatha only. Voice lines in `lines.json` (L1a…L5b). Location: his village house – the
thinnai at sunset (Day 1 plate) and his kattil (rope cot) in the courtyard (owner's 07_home art) from night to morning.
Frames `make_frames.py`; night/dawn/morning light, breathing and the close-ups `tools/toon/morning.py`; sounds
`tools/sfx.py` (alarm bell, snoring, ticks synthesised; the rooster is the CC0 recording).

|---|---|---|
| 1 Big announcement | "Innikku seekiramaa thoongap poren makkaley! Naalaikku kaalaila sooriyan varradhukku munnadiye ezhundhuruven! En discipline-a paarunga!" | f_dsun: sunset on the thinnai, a big yawn (hand at his mouth); f_dpoint: pointing at the made-up kattil |

| 5 The Thatha twist | "Unmaiya sollanumna… alarm-a naan dhaan niruthinen! Naalaikku alarm-a konjam dhooramaa vechukkanum!" | standing by the kattil, blanket folded; finger at his temple; the clock moved far away on the verandah; laugh, pull-out |

Changes from the written directions (no GPU): he sleeps on a kattil in the courtyard (a common village habit) – there
is no house-interior art and code-drawn rooms looked cheap; stretching, lying down, folding the blanket are cuts
between frames; the "Cock-a-doodle-doo!" is the real rooster sound instead of the voice; the switch-off is a close-up
of his arm pressing the clock.
