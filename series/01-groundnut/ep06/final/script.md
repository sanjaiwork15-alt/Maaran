# Groundnut Series – Episode 6: The Groundnut's Underground Trick! 😲🌱

Maaran Thatha | 40–50 s | same field as Episodes 1–5 | script by the owner (2026-10-17)

1. **Continuation (0–7 s)** – (1 s pause) "Pona episode-la namma verkadalai chediyila manjal poo vandhadha paathom la? Aana andha poo-va thaandi, ippo oru aachariyamaana vishayam nadakka pogudhu!" – the flowering plants; Thatha points at a flower, then gestures to the soil.
2. **The secret begins (7–18 s)** – "Indha poovula magarantha serkkai nadandha piragu, adhan adippagudhiyilirundhu oru chinna paguthi valara aarambikkum pa! Adhukku peru peg!" – pollinated flower close-up; the peg starts growing down from the flower's base.
3. **Down into the soil (18–30 s)** – "Indha peg enna pannum theriyuma pa? Mela valaraama, mannai nokki keezha valarum! Mannukkulla pona piragudhaan verkadalai kaai uruvaaga aarambikkum!" – cut-away above and below the soil: the peg enters the soil, a small pod starts.
4. **Fun moment (30–39 s)** – "Adhaan pa… mela ninnaa poo dhaan theriyum! Aana namma verkadalai kaai ellaam mannukkulla ragasiyamaa valarudhu! Enna oru thandhiram!" – Thatha points at the ground, knowing smile; brief view of the underground pods.
5. **Teaser (39–48 s)** – "Ippo mannukkulla kaai uruvaaga aarambichiruchu pa! Aana adhu eppo parikkura alavukku valarum? Adha eppadi kandupidikkuradhu? Adutha episode-la adha paappom pa!" – he examines a plant; a more mature plant; back to Thatha pointing at the field.
