# Groundnut Series – Episode 4: Thatha, Something Is Growing! 🌱

Maaran Thatha | YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels | 40–50 s | same field as Episodes 1–3
**Status:** script by the owner (2026-10-16).

## Scene 1 – The First Sprout (0–8 sec)
VO: (1-second pause) "Enna pa... namma vidhai potta idathula pachai pachaiyaa thalir vandhirukku! (excited) Ahaa... namma uzhaippukku mudhal palan vandhuduchu pa!"
Animation: Close-up of tiny seedlings coming out of the soil; pull back to Thatha, who bends down, sees them and smiles with real excitement.

## Scene 2 – Thatha's Happiness (8–18 sec)
VO: "Chinna vidhai mannukkulla irundhu... ippadi thalira veliya varradha paakkumbodhu manasukku evlo sandhoshama irukku theriyuma pa? (warm chuckle)"
Animation: He kneels by the seedlings, points at a tiny sprout without touching it, then smiles proudly at the camera.

## Scene 3 – A Little Farming Wisdom (18–30 sec)
VO: "Ippo indha chinna chedikku sariyana eerappadhamum, gavanippum mukkiyam pa! Thanni romba adhigama irundhaalum sari, romba kammiya irundhaalum sari... chedi kashtappadum!"
Animation: He checks the soil moisture and waters the seedlings gently with a small can – no water pooling.

## Scene 4 – A Funny Thatha Moment (30–39 sec)
VO: "Indha chinna chediya paathadhum, naalaikke verkadalai pudungidalaam-nu nenachidaadha pa! (playful chuckle) Innum neriya valaranum!"
Animation: He pretends he might pull a seedling, shakes his head and laughs, points at the young plants.

## Scene 5 – Continuation to Episode 5 (39–48 sec)
VO: "Ippo thalir vandhaachu... aduthu indha chedi eppadi valarudhu-nu paappom! Konja naal-la indha chediyila azhagaana manjal poogkal varum pa! Adhula oru suvaarsyamaana ragasiyam irukku... adutha episode-la solren!"
Animation: He gestures at the field; brief preview of a grown plant with small yellow flowers; back to Thatha smiling.
