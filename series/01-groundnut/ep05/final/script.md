# Groundnut Series – Episode 5: These Tiny Flowers Have a Secret! 🌼

Maaran Thatha | 40–50 s | same field as Episodes 1–4 | script by the owner (2026-10-16)

1. **Continuation (0–7 s)** – (1 s pause) "Pona episode-la namma chinna chinna thalir vandhadha paathom la pa? Ippo paaru... andha chedi-la azhagaana manjal poo vandhirukku!" – the Ep 4 plants, now bigger with small yellow flowers; Thatha among them, proud.
2. **A tiny flower (7–17 s)** – "Paakka romba chinna poo dhaan... aana indha poovukku oru mukkiyamaana velai irukku pa! Enna velai-nu yosikkireengala?" – flower close-up; Thatha points at it, playful.
3. **The secret (17–30 s)** – "Indha poo-la magarantha serkkai nadandha piragu, adiyila irukkura karuvarai pagudhiyilirundhu oru chinna paguthi valara aarambikkum! Adhukku peru peg pa... adhu mannai nokki keezha pogum!" – after pollination the peg grows from the base of the flower down to the soil.
4. **Fun moment (30–39 s)** – "Matha chedi-la kaai mela thongum... aana namma verkadalai mattum mannukkulla olinjikkum pa! Idhu enna vithiyasamaana chedi pa!" – he searches the leaves for nuts, grins, points down.
5. **Teaser (39–48 s)** – "Aana indha chinna peg mannukkulla ponaa enna aagum theriyuma pa? Adhula dhaan namma verkadalai uruvaaga pogudhu! Adutha episode-la andha ragasiyatha theliva paappom!" – the peg enters the soil; a small pod starts to form underground.
