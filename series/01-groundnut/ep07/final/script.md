# Groundnut Series – Episode 7: Is It Time to Harvest Yet? 🌱🥜

Maaran Thatha | 40–50 s | same field as Episodes 1–6 | script by the owner (2026-10-17)

1. **Continuation (0–7 s)** – (1 s pause) "Pona episode-la namma verkadalai kaai mannukkulla eppadi uruvaagudhunu paathom la pa? Aana ippo oru mukkiyamaana kelvi… indha kaaiya eppo dhaan parikkanum?" – mature plants; Thatha examines them, looks at the soil.
2. **Don't rush! (7–18 s)** – "Chedi perusa valandhutta udaney pudungida koodadhu pa! Verum mela irukkura chediya mattum paathaa podhaadhu… mannukkulla irukkura kaaiyum nalla valandhirukkanum!" – he pretends to pull a plant too early, stops, shakes his head.
3. **How farmers check (18–30 s)** – "Adhaan pa, vivasaayinga oru chediya maadhiri eduthu paarthu, kaai nalla uruvaayirukkaa-nu sothichu paarpaanga! Kaaiyum vidhaiyum nalla valarndhirukkuradhu mukkiyam pa!" – he pulls one sample plant, looks at the pods; immature vs mature pod.
4. **Fun moment (30–39 s)** – "Saapida mattum avasaram kaattura namakku… vivasaayathula konjam porumai romba mukkiyam pa! Nalla vilaichal venumna, nalla neram vara varaikkum kaathirukkanum!" – rubs his stomach, gestures patiently at the field.
5. **Teaser (39–48 s)** – "Sari pa… ippo oru chediya pudungi paarthachu! Adhula evlo verkadalai irukkunnu therinjukkanumaa? Adutha episode-la mannukkulla olinjirukkura aachariyatha nerla paappom!" – he lifts a mature plant; only a glimpse of the pods, then his excited face.
