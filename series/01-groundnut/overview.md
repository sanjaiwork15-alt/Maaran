# Series 01 – Groundnut (Verkadalai)

**Status:** APPROVED
**Location (whole series):** Semman kaadu, Thatha's red-soil field, with the neem tree, the old well and the motor shed. The final episode moves to the house front for cooking.
**Season:** Margazhi pattam, irrigated groundnut sown in December–January (a real Tamil Nadu groundnut season).
**Variety Thatha uses:** VRI 8, a TNAU variety that takes 105–110 days.
**Episode length:** 45–60 seconds each, 8 episodes.
**Title format:** `Groundnut Episode N – Maaran Thatha | <episode name>`

## The story arc

Thatha opens with a mystery that most people don't know: **groundnut flowers above the ground, but the nut grows under the soil.** The whole series follows one crop from bare land to the plate, so viewers watch the same field change from episode to episode. Every episode ends on a question or a small problem that the next episode answers.

---

### Episode 1 – The Secret Under the Soil
- **Hook (0–3s):** Thatha holds up a groundnut pod: "Indha kadalai maram la kaaikudhu nu nenaikureengala? Thappu!"
- **Story:** He introduces himself and his red-soil field. Groundnut likes loose, well-drained red or sandy-loam soil, because the pods have to grow inside it. He says this season he'll grow it in front of us, from start to finish.
- **Cliffhanger:** "Aana first, vidhai… yella vidhaiyum vidhai illa! Edhai edukkanum? Naalaiku solren."

### Episode 2 – Before the Seeds Go In (made 2026-10-15, owner's script)
- Soil preparation: check the soil, remove stones and weeds, loosen it so roots can spread and seeds sprout.
  Cliffhanger: "Oru chinna vidhaiyilirundhu evlo verkadalai kidaikkum-nu paakkalaama?" → Episode 3 (sowing).
  Script and video: `ep02/final/`. The seed-choice/seed-treatment plan below moves into Episode 3.

### (Planned earlier) Choosing the Right Seed
- **Hook:** He drops two kernels in his palm, one plump and one shrivelled: "Indha rendula onnu thaan mulaikum. Edhu?"
- **Story:** Ploughing the field to make the soil fine. He shells pods by hand and keeps only plump, unbroken kernels. Seed treatment the TNAU way: Trichoderma (a friendly fungus that protects the seed) and Rhizobium (friendly bacteria that help the plant take nitrogen from the air), mixed with cooled rice kanji so it sticks.
- **Cliffhanger:** "Ellaam ready. Naalaiku kaalaila, vidhaipu!"

### Episode 3 – Sowing Day
- **Hook:** A crow lands near the seed bag; Thatha shoos it away: "Dei! Unakku illa idhu!"
- **Story:** Rows one foot (30 cm) apart, seeds about a hand-width (10 cm) apart in each row. He covers them with soil and waters right after sowing.
- **Cliffhanger:** He bends down and squints at the soil: "Hmm… inga paarunga… mannu yen udaiyudhu?"

### Episode 4 – First Sprouts and the Weed War
- **Hook:** Tiny green leaves have broken through the soil: "Paarunga! Kuzhandhai porandhuduchu!"
- **Story:** The seedlings come up. Weeds come up too and steal water and food, so he hand-weeds. He mentions that he'll weed again later and why the timing matters.
- **Cliffhanger:** About a month in, a small yellow flower appears: "Indha poo thaan magic. Aana idhu kaaya aagaadhu… appo enna aagum?"

### Episode 5 – The Flower That Goes Underground
- **Hook:** He points at a thin stalk pushing into the soil: "Idho! Poo mannukulla poguthu!"
- **Story:** After flowering, a needle-like "peg" grows down into the soil and the pod forms there. That's why he earths up: he pulls soil up around the plants and adds gypsum (calcium) beside them around day 40–45, so the pods fill properly. After day 45 he doesn't disturb the soil, so he won't break the pegs. Water matters most at flowering, pegging and pod filling.
- **Cliffhanger:** He finds a leaf with strange curling and spots: "Aiyo… yaaro namba payira saapdraanga!"

### Episode 6 – Trouble in the Field
- **Hook:** He lifts a damaged leaf to the camera: "Thirudan vandhuttaan!"
- **Story:** He checks his plants every morning. He identifies the leaf miner (a tiny caterpillar that folds leaves) and explains simple field checks. His lesson is to catch problems early and ask the local agriculture officer before spraying anything; he doesn't spray blindly.
- **Cliffhanger:** A few weeks later the lower leaves are drying: "Payir saagudha? …Illa kanna. Idhu nalla seidhi!"

### Episode 7 – Harvest Day
- **Hook:** He grips a plant: "Moonu maasam wait pannom. Ippo… moment of truth!"
- **Story:** The signs of maturity are that the lower leaves dry and the top leaves turn yellow. He pulls a few plants, cracks a pod, and shows that the inside of the shell has gone dark (not white), which means it's ready. The big pull reveals clusters of pods hanging from the roots.
- **Cliffhanger:** "Pudungiyaachu. Aana ippove saapta, ellaam kettu pogum! Yen?"

### Episode 8 – From Field to Plate
- **Location shift:** Thatha's house front, the only location change, and it's planned.
- **Hook:** He sits on the thinnai with a pile of pods: "Iniku thaan best part!"
- **Story:** He sun-dries the pods well before storing them, because wet pods spoil. Then he shows his favourite ways to eat groundnut (boiled, roasted, kadalai urundai) and says simply that groundnut is rich in protein and good fats. No medical claims.
- **Cliffhanger / teaser:** He looks toward the wet field: "Adutha series? Namba vayal ku poalaam… paappom!" (This sets up the next series.)
