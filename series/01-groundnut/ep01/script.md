# Groundnut Episode 1 – Maaran Thatha | The Secret Under the Soil

**Status:** DRAFT – waiting for your approval
**Length target:** ~50 seconds
**Location:** Semman kaadu (Thatha's red-soil field): neem tree at the edge, old well and motor shed, Arunachala hill faint on the horizon. Early morning light.
**Field state:** Bare, ploughed red soil, before sowing.
**Sound:** Village breeze + distant birds throughout, soft folk tune underneath (dips while he talks).
**On-screen text:** None.

---

## Scene 1 – The Hook (0–4s)

**Shot:** Tight close-up. Thatha holds one groundnut pod right up to the camera and raises an eyebrow.

> **THATHA:** Indha kadalai… marathula kaaikudhu nu nenaikireengala? *(beat)* Thappu!

## Scene 2 – Intro (4–9s)

**Shot:** Camera pulls back to a medium shot. He laughs and taps his walking stick once on the soil.

> **THATHA:** Ha ha! Vaanga vaanga… ennoda peru Maaran Thatha. Aimbadhu varusham, indha mannula thaan en vaazhkai.

## Scene 3 – The Secret (9–18s)

**Shot:** Wide shot of the bare red field; he points with the stick, then down at the ground.

> **THATHA:** Kadalai oru adhisayamaana payir kanna. Poo… mela pookum. Aana kaai? *(leans in, whispers)* Mannukulla thaan urugum!

## Scene 4 – Why this soil (18–30s)

**Shot:** He bends slowly (hand on knee, a small "aiyo" for comedy), picks up a fistful of red soil and lets it crumble through his fingers.

> **THATHA:** Aiyo, mutti… *(chuckles)* Paaru, kai la eduthaa udhirudhu. Indha maadhiri lesaana, semman mannu thaan kadalaiku pudikum. Thanni thenga koodadhu. Kettiyaana mannula, kaai ulla poga mudiyaadhu!

## Scene 5 – The Promise (30–40s)

**Shot:** He straightens up, looks across the field, then back at camera, proud.

> **THATHA:** Indha Margazhi pattathula, naan indha nilathula kadalai poda poren. Vidhai la irundhu, unga thattu varaikum… ellaathaiyum, unga kannu munnaadi kaatren.

## Scene 6 – Cliffhanger (40–50s)

**Shot:** He opens his palm to show a few groundnut kernels, then closes his fist with a mischievous smile. Slow push-in.

> **THATHA:** Aana first… vidhai. Ella vidhaiyum, vidhai illa kanna! Edhai edukkanum, edhai thookki podanum? *(smiles)* Adutha episode la solren… poitu vaanga!

---

## What it means (English)

1. "You think this groundnut grows on a tree? Wrong!"
2. "Ha ha! Come, come… my name is Maaran Thatha. Fifty years, my life has been in this soil."
3. "Groundnut is a wonder crop, dear. The flower blooms up top. But the nut? It grows inside the soil!"
4. "Oh, my knee… Look, pick it up and it crumbles. Groundnut loves light, red soil like this. Water must not stagnate. In hard soil, the nut can't go in!"
5. "This Margazhi season I'm going to plant groundnut in this field. From seed to your plate… I'll show you everything with your own eyes."
6. "But first… the seed. Not every seed is a good seed, dear! Which to take, which to throw away? I'll tell you next episode… bye for now!"
