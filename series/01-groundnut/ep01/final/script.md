# Groundnut Series – Episode 1: Why Do Groundnuts Grow Underground? 🌱

Maaran Thatha | YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels | Target duration: 45–50 seconds
**Status:** script by the owner; video done and approved (`groundnut_ep01.mp4`).

Episode objective: Hook viewers with a surprising question, introduce Maaran Thatha's charming personality,
reveal a little-known groundnut fact, and make viewers curious about the next episode.

## Scene 1 — The Hook (0–5 sec)
Voice-over: "Dei pa... oru kelvi! (playful pause) Verkadalai chedi-la kaaykkudha... illa mannukku keezha kaaykkudha? (curious tone) Therinja sollunga pa!"
Animation: Maaran Thatha stands in his farm, holding a few groundnuts in his palm. He brings them close to the camera, raises one eyebrow, and gives viewers a mischievous smile. He points his walking stick toward the soil.

## Scene 2 — Meet Maaran Thatha (5–13 sec)
Voice-over: "Naan dhaan unga Maaran Thatha pa! (warm chuckle) Indha chinna verkadalai-kku pinnadi oru periya ragasiyame irukku!"
Animation: Thatha taps his chest proudly when introducing himself, then holds up a groundnut between his fingers. He leans slightly toward the camera as if sharing a village secret.

## Scene 3 — The Surprise (13–27 sec)
Voice-over: "Namma saapadra verkadalai... chedi mela thongittu irukkaadhu pa! Indha chedi poo vidum... adhukku appuram oru chinna paguthi mannukkulla pogum! Anga dhaan verkadalai kaai uruvaagum! Aachariyama irukku illa?"
Animation: Show a healthy groundnut plant growing above the soil. A yellow flower appears. Transition to a simple underground cutaway animation showing a small peg growing downward into the soil, where groundnut pods begin to develop. Return to Thatha smiling and nodding.

## Scene 4 — Thatha's Personality (27–38 sec)
Voice-over: "Mela paathaa saadhaarana chedi maadhiri dhaan irukkum... Aana mukkiyamaana velai ellaam mannukku keezha nadakkudhu pa! (amused chuckle) Vivasayam-na summa illa!"
Animation: Thatha looks at the plant, then points dramatically toward the soil. He shakes his head with a knowing smile and laughs gently. Briefly show the plant above ground and the developing pods below ground.

## Scene 5 — Cliffhanger into Episode 2 (38–48 sec)
Voice-over: "Sari pa... ippadi oru arputhamaana payir valaranumna, mudhalla enna pannanum theriyuma? Adutha episode-la namma nilatha thayaar panna porom! Vaanga pa... velaiya aarambippom!"
Animation: Thatha gestures toward a patch of unprepared soil, picks up his farming tool, and walks toward it with an enthusiastic expression. End with him turning toward the camera and smiling. Transition to the same field, ready for soil preparation in Episode 2.
