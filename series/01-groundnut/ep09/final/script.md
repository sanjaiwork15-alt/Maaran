# Groundnut Series – Episode 9: Why Can't We Eat Them Straight Away? 🤔🥜

Maaran Thatha | 40–50 s | same farm as Episodes 1–8 | script by the owner (2026-10-18)

1. **The question (0–7 s)** – (1 s pause) "Seri makkaley! Mannula irundhu verkadalaiya eduthuttaachu… appo udane saapidalamaa? Konjam porunga!" – he looks at a fresh pod as if to open it, pauses, turns to the camera.
2. **Fresh from the soil (7–16 s)** – "Ippo dhaan mannula irundhu eduthirukkom. Idhula mann ottirukkum, kaayum eeramaa irukkum. Mudhalla nallaa sutham pannanum!" – shakes soil off the pods; close-up of soiled fresh pods.
3. **Why drying matters (16–27 s)** – "Saemichu vaikka poromnaa, sariyaa ularavaikkaradhu romba mukkiyam! Eeram adhigamaa irundhaa poonjai valaralaam… verkadalaiyum kettupogalaam!" – pods in a thin layer on a mat in the sun; he checks them; damp pods → mould.
4. **Funny lesson (27–37 s)** – "Avvalavu avasaramaa irukkaadheenga! Nalla saapidanumna konjam porumai venum. Ularndha verkadalaiya varuthaa… semma suvai dhaan!" – playful gesture; brief look at a bowl of roasted groundnuts.
5. **Final teaser (37–47 s)** – "Idhu varaikkum oru chinna vidhai eppadi verkadalaiyaa valarudhunnu paathom! Adutha episode-la, indha verkadalai namma tea-time snack-aa eppadi maarudhunnu paakkalaam!" – he holds the bowl proudly; close-up of the bowl. (No on-screen title – owner's no-text rule.)
