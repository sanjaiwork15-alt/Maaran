# Groundnut Series – Episode 2: Before the Seeds Go In… 🌱

Maaran Thatha | YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels | Target duration: 40–50 seconds
**Status:** script by the owner (2026-10-15); video made with the Ep 1 method.
**Location:** the same groundnut field (Semman kaadu) as Episode 1.

> Note: the series overview planned "Choosing the Right Seed" for Ep 2. The owner's script makes Ep 2 about
> preparing the soil (as Ep 1's cliffhanger promised) and moves the seed to Ep 3.

## Scene 1 – Hook (0–6 sec)
Voice-over: "Vidhai nalla irundhaa mattum podhaadhu pa! (pause) Mannum nalla irukkanum! Illana namma paadu dhaan waste! (playful expression)"
Animation: Maaran Thatha stands in the same farm, holding a handful of groundnut seeds. He looks at the seeds, then at the soil, and shakes his head playfully. He points his walking stick toward the ground.

## Scene 2 – Checking the Soil (6–16 sec)
Voice-over: "Adhaan vidhai poduradhukku munnadi, indha mannai nalla thayaar pannanum pa! Mannai nalla kilarri, kal um pullum irundhaa eduthudanum!"
Animation: Thatha bends down, picks up a little soil, and rubs it between his fingers. Show him removing a few stones and weeds from the field. Keep his movements simple and natural.

## Scene 3 – Thatha's Farming Wisdom (16–29 sec)
Voice-over: "Verkadalai valaranumna, mannukkulla veru nalla parava idam venum pa! Mannai sariyaa thayaar panninaa, vidhai mulaikkavum chedi valaravum udhaviya irukkum!"
Animation: Show Thatha loosening the soil with a simple farming tool. Cut to a close-up of the loose soil, then show him looking over the prepared patch with a satisfied smile.

## Scene 4 – A Little Thatha Humour (29–38 sec)
Voice-over: "Naamalaam saapida ukkaandhaa udaney saapidanum-nu aasapaduvom! (chuckles) Aana vivasaayathula ellaathukkum oru neram irukku pa!"
Animation: Thatha pats his stomach with a humorous smile, then gestures patiently toward the field. He gives the camera a knowing look and chuckles.

## Scene 5 – Continuation to Episode 3 (38–48 sec)
Voice-over: "Sari pa... nilam thayaar aayiduchu! Aduthu indha chinna vidhaiya mannukkulla poda porom. Oru chinna vidhaiyilirundhu evlo verkadalai kidaikkum-nu paakkalaama? Adutha episode-la sandhippom pa!"
Animation: Thatha stands beside the prepared soil, holds up a groundnut seed, and smiles at the camera. He kneels and prepares to plant it. End with a close-up of the seed in his hand before transitioning to Episode 3.
