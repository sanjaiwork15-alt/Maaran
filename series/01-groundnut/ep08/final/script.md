# Groundnut Series – Episode 8: Look What's Hiding Under the Soil! 😲🥜

Maaran Thatha | 40–50 s | same field as Episodes 1–7 | script by the owner (2026-10-18)

1. **The big reveal (0–8 s)** – (1 s pause) "Ada paavi! Ivlo verkadalai mannukku ulla irukka? Veliyila paatha onnum theriyave illaye!" – he pulls the mature plant from Ep 7; clusters of pods on the roots; surprise, then a proud smile; close-up of the pods.
2. **The hidden treasure (8–17 s)** – "Paathingala makkaley! Indha kaayellam dhaan mannukku adiyila uruvaagudhu. Ovoru kaayaiyum paarunga… sila perusa irukku, sila innum chinnadha irukku!" – holds the plant to the camera; pods of different sizes.
3. **A farmer's lesson (17–27 s)** – "Idha dhaan vivasaayi porumaiyaa valarthirukkaaru! Aana ellaa kaayum ore alavula irukkaadhu. Mannu, thanni, ragam… ellaame mukkiyam!" – soil, roots, pods.
4. **Funny moment (27–37 s)** – "Enna, kadaikku poi vaanguradha vida namma veettula valarthaa nallaa irukkum pola irukke! Aana vivasaayam-na uzhaippum venum, porumaiyum venum!" – holds up a pod like a prize, chuckles, nods.
5. **Next surprise (37–47 s)** – "Aana makkaley, indha verkadalaiya parichadhum udane saapida mudiyumaa? Adhukkum oru mukkiyamaana vishayam irukku! Adha adutha episode-la paakkalaam!" – close-up of fresh pods; cut before the next step (drying).
