# Groundnut Series – Episode 10 (finale): From Tiny Seed to Tea-Time Snack! 😋🥜

Maaran Thatha | 40–50 s | farm + Thatha's house (the series' planned final location) | script by the owner (2026-10-18)

1. **The final journey (0–8 s)** – (1 s pause) "Vanakkam makkaley! Oru chinna vidhaiya mannula pottu aarambichom… ippo adhu namma tea-time snack-a maara pogudhu!" – quick recap: seed, sprout, flowers, harvest; back to Thatha.
2. **Clean and dry (8–17 s)** – "Vayal-la irundhu edutha verkadalaiya mudhalla sutham pannanum. Saemichu vaikka poromnaa, poonjai varaama irukka nallaa ularavaikkanum!" – cleaned pods dried in a thin layer.
3. **Time to roast (17–27 s)** – "Ippo indha ularndha verkadalaiya sariyaa varuthaa… andha vaasanaiye vera level dhaan! Aana adhigamaa karugaama gavanamaa varukkanum!" – village kitchen, iron kadai, careful stirring with a long ladle (no careless handling of the hot pan).
4. **The first bite (27–37 s)** – "Aahaa! Idhudhaan namma uzhaippoda palan! Oru chinna vidhaiyila irundhu, ivlo azhagaana payanam… vivasaayathula ovvoru kattamum mukkiyam!" – sitting on the thinnai with a bowl, tastes one, smiles.
5. **Series finale (37–48 s)** – "Indha verkadalai payanam ungalukku pudichirundhaa, Maaran Thatha channel-a Subscribe pannunga! Adutha payiroda innoru pudhu payanathula sandhippom. Nandri makkaley!" – waves warmly; cheerful farm view. (No on-screen finale card – owner's no-text rule.)
