## Fact check
- Compost (organic manure) improves soil structure, water holding and aeration, and feeds soil life – correct;
  TNAU Agritech Portal (organic farming / compost; groundnut recommends well-decomposed FYM or compost before sowing).
- "Nalla prepare panni" – mature, well-decomposed compost: correct. Fresh, unfinished compost heats up and can
  lock up nitrogen as it keeps breaking down, and can harm seedlings – so it should be mature before use.
- "Sariyana alavula" – correct: dumping a huge pile on one spot is wasteful and can smother the soil; spread it
  evenly and mix it into the top soil at recommended rates (TNAU gives per-hectare doses for groundnut).
- The caterpillar cliffhanger is generic (no species claim) – Ep 3 can name it.
