## Fact check
- Seeds need moisture (and air, warmth) to germinate; hard, dry, compacted soil holds little water and air, and a
  crust makes it hard for seedlings to come up – "vidhai valaradhukku thanni mattum podhuma? Mannum nalla
  irukkanume!": correct. Groundnut needs loose, friable, well-drained soil with good moisture at sowing (TNAU
  Agritech Portal – Groundnut: soil, field preparation, sowing; FAO notes on soil compaction).
- Germination takes several days (groundnut usually about a week, depending on moisture and temperature), so
  "adutha naal… payir valandhirukkanum" is the joke, and the episode says so ("porumai").
- "Oru maasathula car" is set up as Thatha's overconfidence – not advice.
