# Maaran Thatha's World

## Home village

A small, fictional farming village in **Tiruvannamalai district**, northern Tamil Nadu. This region really grows groundnut, paddy, sugarcane, millets, pulses, banana and vegetables, so every food series can come from the same village.

The village never changes. Each **series** has its own fixed spot on Thatha's land, and that spot stays the same for every episode of that series.

## Thatha's land (series locations)

| Spot | Description | Used for series like |
|---|---|---|
| **Semman kaadu** (red-soil dry field) | Red sandy-loam field, a big neem tree at the edge, an old open well with a motor shed, Arunachala hill faint on the horizon | Groundnut, millets, pulses, sesame |
| **Vayal** (wet field) | Flooded paddy plots beside the village lake, a small bund path | Paddy/rice, sugarcane |
| **Thottam** (garden plot) | Banana, coconut, vegetable beds near the house | Banana, coconut, brinjal, chilli, etc. |
| **Veedu** (house front) | Thinnai verandah, cow shed, drying yard | Final "field to plate" episodes, cooking, storage |
